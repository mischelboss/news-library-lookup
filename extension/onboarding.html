Spiegel via VÖBB

This extension adds a link to paid articles on the Spiegel homepage. The link opens the matching article in VÖBB's Genios service, where your library card decides whether you can read it.

Set it up

  1. Sign in to VÖBB Genios ↗ in this Firefox profile. A private window or container may not share the session.
  2. Check the access below.
  3. Open or reload spiegel.de ↗.

What happens with your data

  • For each paid headline on the homepage, the extension sends the headline's words to VÖBB Genios from your browser, using your own signed-in session. Genios may log these searches. This is the "website content" that Firefox lists when you install.
  • The extension never reads or stores your password, cookies, or tokens.
  • Results are cached on this device for up to six hours and cleared from the popup. They can reveal what you read, and they do not sync anywhere.
  • Nothing is sent to the developer or to anyone other than Genios.

Good to know

  • A green link means a strong title match. Your library still decides whether you may read the full text.
  • Searches run one at a time with short pauses, and stop for a minute after a failure. Please do not try to speed this up.
  • Use the toolbar icon to pause checks, retry failed ones, or clear the cache.
